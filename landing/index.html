How territory works Orientation season

Exploit psychology

Nobody keeps running on discipline alone. Six pressures feed the same habit, and the app is built to keep all six switched on at once.

Seasons

Runner's Inferno

Intergroup competition tends to sustain running motivation. Our seasonals last 3 weeks and put you in a random group with other strangers. Run at your own time, no meetup friction, wherever you want.

A season runs two boards. One ranks every runner who entered against every other, so your standing is your place among all of them. The other ranks the groups of six against each other, which is the part your own group is playing for.

Rundating · optional

Bounties help us finance the app

Entering a season is free. Men can pay a bounty on top of that to be guaranteed a mixed group. The bounty splits in half: half funds the app, and half goes to the top women runners in the season when it closes.*

* Top is measured on distance covered and how often you ran across the season. Where the bar sits stays private, because a payout rule published in full is a payout rule someone works out how to farm.

Conquer nodes with frequency

Nodes are held not just because you ran further, but through repeated runs over the same trail. Steal nodes from other runners.

1

Run, and the grid scores it

Your route is laid over a hexagonal grid, the same one Uber's H3 system uses. Each cell picks up a score weighted by how much of it you covered.

2

Come back, and you own it

Ownership is winner-takes-all per cell. Whoever holds the highest live score holds the cell outright, which is why running the same streets beats running new ones.

3

Stay away, and you lose it

Scores decay nightly. A month off a corridor hands it back to whoever is still out there. Territory that cannot be lost is not worth taking.

Sample territory over Jurong. 32 nodes held, 211 contested.

Influence

Showing up in hard ground still counts

Winner-takes-all means second place across a whole neighbourhood scores nothing, which punishes anyone who runs somewhere busy. Influence counts every cell you have a live score in, held or not, and the leaderboard sorts on its 30-day average.

Map

Watch your streets turn your colour

Run nodes to contest them. Leave them alone for a while and the colour drains back out. When the nodes around a street are yours as well, the whole area deepens, which is how a stronghold ends up looking different from one lucky detour.

Interior Research

Every number on this page comes out of the same set of findings. Swipe through, or leave it and it moves on its own.

12s faster

You run faster with company

Researchers timed parkrun regulars across repeated 5K runs. The ones who felt most part of the group came in up to 12 seconds quicker, and rated the effort no harder than usual.

Davis et al., PLOS ONE 2021
The Köhler effect
2× as long

A team keeps you going

When the group score depends on your effort, you last about twice as long as you manage alone. The effect needs your contribution to be indispensable, which is why a group is six.

Irwin & Feltz, Ann Behav Med 2012
1.1 million runners, 5 years
0.3km

Your friends drag your distance up

Every extra kilometre your friends ran on a given day added roughly 0.3 km to your own run that day. Weather was used as a natural experiment to separate influence from shared habits.

Aral & Nicolaides, Nature Comms 2017
42 studies, 1,843 participants
↓dropout

Walking groups hold people

A meta-analysis of organised walking groups found reductions in blood pressure, resting heart rate, body fat, cholesterol, BMI and depression risk, with few adverse effects and low dropout.

Hanson & Jones, Br J Sports Med 2015
247 older adults, 6 months
↑support

Moving with others builds belonging

Perceived social support rose while stress and loneliness fell. The increase in support predicted the drop in loneliness both directly and through the reduction in stress.

Aging Clin Exp Res, 2021
Pain threshold as endorphin proxy
↑threshold

Effort itself does some of the bonding

Rowers training as a crew showed higher pain thresholds than after equivalent solo work. A later dance study found synchrony and exertion each raise pain threshold and in-group bonding on their own.

Cohen et al., Biol Lett 2010 · Tarr et al., Biol Lett 2015
Randomized controlled trial
+2%

Walking regrows the brain

Sedentary adults walked 40 minutes, three times a week, for a year. Their hippocampus grew by about 2%, winding back one to two years of age-related shrinkage.

Erickson et al., PNAS 2011
34,485 adults over 65
0.1m/s

Walking speed predicts survival

Every 0.1 m/s of walking pace tracked with better odds of survival. Age, sex and pace together forecast lifespan about as well as blood pressure, BMI and disease history do.

Studenski et al., JAMA 2011
Bone loss in spaceflight
1–2% / month

Bone needs to be loaded

Astronauts lose one to two percent of bone mineral density a month in orbit, roughly ten times the monthly rate of postmenopausal osteoporosis on Earth. The cause is the absence of load, not age.

npj Microgravity, 2020
The system with no heart
↑clearance

Muscle is the pump for everything that is not blood

Lymph clears waste out of your tissue and has no heart of its own. It moves when skeletal muscle squeezes the vessels running through it, and tracer cleared faster during dynamic movement than at rest.

Havas et al., J Physiol 1997
Evolutionary anatomy
2Myears

The body is built for this

A spring-like Achilles, an arched foot, a large gluteus maximus that fires in locomotion, and millions of sweat glands that cool you while you keep moving. These are derived traits of genus Homo.

Bramble & Lieberman, Nature 2004
