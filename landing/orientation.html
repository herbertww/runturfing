Your school against
every other school.

Sixteen institutions are on the board: six universities, five polytechnics, the three ITE colleges, NAFA and LASALLE. You opt into one. From then on the ground you take is counted twice, once for you and once for the crest.

Ranked on ground held. Nothing is divided.

A school's score is the total turf its members hold, with distance breaking ties. Runner counts are shown next to it and take no part in it.

Why not an average

One fast runner should not win a season

Dividing by runners looks fairer until you play it out. A school that fields one strong runner and nobody else posts a per-runner average nothing can touch, and every further entrant can only pull it down. That turns inviting a schoolmate into an act of self-harm.

What totalling buys

Every runner you add moves you up

On a total, an extra entrant never costs a place. Someone who signs up and runs once helps; someone who signs up and never starts costs nothing. Larger campuses do have an advantage, and that is the trade being made: this season rewards getting people out.

The lock

Your pick freezes when the season starts

Switch schools as often as you like until the opening whistle. After that the crest is fixed for the season. Without the freeze, a runner could spend two weeks taking ground for one campus and hand the whole holding to a rival on the final evening.
