WHY IT WORKS

Wanting to run has never been the problem.

Humans evolved to conserve energy, which is why willpower loses so reliably. Runfluence works around it with three social pulls, arranged so that at least one of them is active on any given day.

Async · copresence

A group of six, nothing to schedule

Your group runs whenever they want, and you see all of it: their routes, their claims, where they stand. The sense of company arrives without anyone coordinating a meetup.

Removes coordination, the barrier that ends most running habits, and keeps the belonging that sustains them.

Walking-group meta-analysis, BJSM 2015
Indispensability

Your team can't win without you

Groups compete against other groups, and every run feeds one shared score. Five people notice when you go out, and the same five notice when you don't.

The Köhler effect: people exercise measurably longer once the team depends on them.

Irwin & Feltz, Ann Behav Med 2012
Loss aversion

Territory you can actually lose

Running the same corridors claims map cells for you. Stop defending them and they fade back out. Losing ground you already earned registers about twice as strongly as gaining it.

Puts your body's use-it-or-lose-it biology somewhere you can watch it happen.

Exercise contagion, Nature Comms 2017
